# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

Be respectful, assume good faith, give actionable technical feedback, and
accept responsibility for mistakes. Keep discussions focused on the work and
make room for contributors with different backgrounds and levels of expertise.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized
comments, and publishing private information without permission are not
acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report a conduct concern privately to the repository maintainers listed in the
organization profile or through the security contact in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict
participation when needed to protect the community. Reports are handled
confidentially and retaliation is not tolerated.
